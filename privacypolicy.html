Medical Information

Google Single Sign-On (SSO)

We may provide an option for users to log in using their Google account. If you choose this option, we may collect your email address from Google solely to authenticate your identity and provide access to our services. You may revoke our access to your Google account at any time through your Google account settings.

We may request access or permission to track location-based information from your mobile device, either continuously or while using our mobile applications, to provide location-based services such as home sample collection coordination and technician visit updates. You may change permissions in your device settings.

How We Use Your Information

  • Schedule home sample collection appointments
  • Coordinate phlebotomist visits
  • Process and transport samples to partner laboratories
  • Deliver diagnostic reports
  • Process payments and invoices
  • Provide customer support
  • Send appointment reminders and service notifications
  • Improve our services and website performance
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Assist with order management, ETA updates, delivery notifications, order alerts, and refunds where applicable

Partner Laboratory Services

RNL Labs provides home sample collection and healthcare logistics services. Diagnostic testing and report generation are performed by our partner laboratory: Medclin Path Labs Private Limited.

Only the minimum necessary information required for testing and reporting is shared with partner laboratories.

WhatsApp, SMS & Email Communications

By providing your contact information and using our services, you consent to receive:

  • Appointment confirmations

  • Technician arrival notifications

  • Billing receipts
  • Diagnostic reports
  • Service-related communications

Reports may be delivered through WhatsApp, email, SMS links, or our digital platforms.
